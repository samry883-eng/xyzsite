The Directors

Dris Yousif

Director / Co-Founder
Qatar · CG / Hybrid

Built from the inside.

Dris Yousif is a director and co-founder of XYZ Studios. With a background in CG and VFX, he approaches film from both the creative and production sides, developing direction, design and execution as one continuous process.

Being embedded in the studio keeps him close to the work from the first treatment through the final image, allowing ideas to evolve without losing the intention they started with. His work leans toward constructed worlds, product-led imagery and films that sit somewhere between photographed and designed.

Lenn Antón

Director
Berlin · Live Action / Hybrid

Between the digital and the real.

Lenn Antón is a director raised on music videos, film and 3D. His work lives between the digital and the real, combining live-action filmmaking with the instincts of someone who understands CG from the inside.

Coming up through music videos shaped a fast, instinctive approach to image-making that now carries into his commercial work. Movement, texture, transitions and digital techniques become part of the film language without losing the physicality of what was captured in camera.

XYZ is a creatively led production studio for commercial film, working from early direction and production through CG, VFX, sound and final finish.

We work closely with our partners to understand what the idea is really trying to do, then shape the visual language around it. Whether we’re making the film from the start or stepping in along the way, the intention stays the same: to add something meaningful to the work and carry that thinking through to the final image.
