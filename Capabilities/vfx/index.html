Case study
Storyboard
Rory McIlroy
RBC Canadian Open

With no live shoot possible, the client asked us to develop the creative direction and bring the concept to life.

We crafted a stylized 3D hype reel, taking the project from concept art through to final execution. Throughout the process, the client remained closely involved, providing notes and feedback as we shaped the visuals to capture Rory’s momentum and the anticipation around the tournament.

Pre-visualization
Rory McIlroy
RBC Canadian Open

Before moving into final production, we built a full pre-vis to map out pacing, camera moves, and overall flow. This stage gave the client a clear first look at how the story would unfold on screen.

By reviewing timing, framing, and energy early on, they were able to provide detailed notes and shape the direction with us. The pre-vis ensured everyone was aligned creatively before resources went into final rendering and polish.

Asset Prep
Rory McIlroy
RBC Canadian Open

The client asked that specific elements be featured within the reel to tie back to Rory and the tournament. We developed 3D assets for the Osprey bird (a nod to the course), Rory's championship ring, the custom Rory gold ball, and the RBC Canadian Open trophy.

Each was designed and integrated to enhance the narrative while aligning with the overall creative direction.

Case study
Environment
Rory McIlroy
RBC Canadian Open

Since no shoot was possible, we relied on archival footage of Rory and rebuilt the tournament setting digitally. Using detailed golf course maps as a base, we laid out and modeled the landscapes of Osprey Valley.

From there, we re-created and enhanced the textures, rebuilt surfaces, and scattered nature elements to bring the environments to life. The result was a set of realistic, fully CG environments that replicated the real course while still supporting the stylized, cinematic direction of the reel.
