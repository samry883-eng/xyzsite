Starting Eleven

XYZ Studios was brought on to handle visual effects for a high-energy promotional piece created for CONCACAF and the 2025 Gold Cup, filmed on location at SoFi Stadium. We handled full 3D asset creation, simulation, and crowd integration — re-creating the official match ball in CG, developing the opening particle sim, populating SoFi Stadium with CG crowds, and integrating effects into archival footage. We led all stages of the pipeline, from animation to simulation, rendering, compositing, and final polish.

01  /  Particle Simulation

Developed a custom particle simulation tied to the motion of a soccer ball for the film's opening — a dynamic entry point into the CONCACAF world, setting the tone for the rest of the trailer.

02  /  Ball Recreation

We fully re-created the official tournament ball in 3D, modeled from reference photos to match the real-world version with complete accuracy.

03  /  Archival Footage Integration

The client asked us to extend the effect to archival match footage — adapting the sim to overlay and interact with pre-existing shots, blending old footage with new energy.

04  /  CG Crowds

The original shoot at SoFi Stadium featured entirely empty stands. We fully populated the stadium using CG crowds to match the energy of a major tournament.

05  /  Stadium Model / Camera Tracking

We licensed a detailed 3D model of SoFi Stadium and aligned it with production footage using tracked camera data — accurately filling stands while preserving real-world angles and lighting.

06  /  Cleanup / Polish

We took on select cleanup tasks throughout — refining details, removing visual distractions, and ensuring a consistently polished final look across all shots.
