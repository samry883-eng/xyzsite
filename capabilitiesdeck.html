What We Do

Creative Direction

We translate the client's vision into a clear VFX approach that fits the brand, the brief, and the level of realism the project needs.

Full-Shot Execution

We take ownership from matchmove and layout through asset build, simulation, and CG integration — keeping shots organized and review-ready.

Beauty + Cleanup

Roto, paint, polish, and continuity fixes treated with the same care as main VFX so the shot feels finished, not touched.

Research + Look Development

We explore references, test techniques, and build early look development to lock the visual direction before cameras roll.

Environments + Simulation

Digital environments and extensions, paired with simulations that add scale, realism, and physical detail where it matters.

AI-Assisted Workflows

We integrate AI where it improves speed and iteration, while keeping realism and control intact. A tool, not a replacement.

Technical Planning

We align with production on on-set capture needs — prepping the team with shot notes, tracking and lens data, and lighting reference.

Compositing + Paint

Balancing light, texture, and continuity for a seamless final frame — dialed for realism, clarity, and consistency shot-to-shot.

Sound

Full sound production from sound design through mix and composition, with original foley when needed.
