XYZSTUDIOS
Founded through the creative partnership of producers Dris and Samry, XYZ Studios is an independent post-production studio built on trust, craft, and a shared instinct for thoughtful execution. What began as a close collaboration has evolved into a unified team aligned by taste, precision, and a deep respect for the work.
Working across visual effects, CGI, editing, sound, and advanced production technology, we collaborate with directors, agencies, and production teams to translate ideas into visual storytelling — staying hands-on from concept through final delivery to ensure each project receives the attention to detail it deserves.
